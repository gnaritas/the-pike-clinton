New Haven-style.

Clinton already knows what a New Haven-style pie is. We’re putting the guy who made them here for a decade behind the oven — and adding the thick, airy Sicilian his family grew up on.

Full Menu Get Directions

1New Haven-style — thin, crisp, neighborhood-native

Co-owner Joseph Giordano made New Haven-style pizza in this building for ten years. The pies at The Pike are the continuation of that: rolled thin, cooked hard, made the way this town learned to order them.

2Grandma-style — the owners’ name for it

The other pie is what Jake Mastroianni calls “grandma style”: a Sicilian/Detroit-esque pan pizza — thick and airy with the crisp corners everyone fights over last slice. Sheet-cut into squares, built for sharing.

After midnight, the pie keeps going.

Friday, Saturday and Sunday, slices stay out until 1 a.m. alongside drinks, wings and fried pickles — Clinton’s late table just got a pizza rail.
