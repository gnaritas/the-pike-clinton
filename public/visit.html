Questions people ask

When does The Pike open?

The Pike opens in November 2026 at 36 Killingworth Turnpike in Clinton, Connecticut. The opening-day post goes out first on Instagram and Facebook.

How late are you open?

Friday, Saturday and Sunday the bar runs until 1 a.m. — cocktails, pizza by the slice, wings and fried pickles. Full daytime hours are announced at opening.

Is it family-friendly?

Yes. The Pike was built as a family restaurant first — pizza, smashburgers and fried chicken for everybody — and the bar scene takes over late on weekends.

Where exactly is it?

36 Killingworth Turnpike, Clinton, CT 06413, in the building that housed Chow Food and Beverage for ten years.
