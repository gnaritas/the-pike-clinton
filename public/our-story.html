Two Families. This Address.

Clinton roots,
a rebuilt room

The Pike is the second restaurant for Jake Mastroianni and the newest chapter for Joe Giordano, who spent a decade making pizza inside these exact walls. They’re cousins. They’re both on the line.

The Pizza Late Nights

The partners

Jake Mastroianni — a Clinton native, 30 years old — already runs Bacari Social in Westbrook and helped build Clinton’s J&M Dockside. The Pike is his second restaurant, and he’s not just the guy with the lease: he cooks on the line with his business partner.

Joseph Giordano is the pizza. He made New Haven-style pies at this address through the last decade at Chow Food and Beverage, and at The Pike he drives the pizza program — the New Haven-style rolls and the thick Sicilian his family calls “grandma style.”

The ground it stands on

The 1,100-square-foot room was Chow Food and Beverage for ten years — a stop owned by Jake’s father, Jeff Mastroianni, and his uncle. When it closed in 2026, the cousins took the space and rebuilt it from the studs out: dark-tiled bar under nine-foot white marble, a pool table, games on seven or eight screens, everything stripped back to what the night actually needs.

What they’re building

The plan is specific, not scattered: pizza two ways, smashburgers and fried chicken priced so a full dinner doesn’t feel like an event, and a bar serious about cocktails — open to families at six and open to the town until one on weekends.

“We’re just going to give you really good food, be really nice, make some pretty special cocktails…we will definitely be trying to elevate the cocktail scene.”— Jake Mastroianni, Hartford Courant

Follow the opening

Doors open in November 2026. Day-by-day plans, hours and the full menu go out on Instagram and Facebook first — that’s where the town watches.
