After Dark · Fri–Sun Till 1

The bar stays open
until 1 a.m.

“There’s very few places open past midnight to have a drink in town,” co-owner Jake Mastroianni says. So The Pike is one of them — cocktails, pitchers, slices and the fryer, Friday through Sunday.

Full Menu Get Directions

1The pour

Craft cocktails made with intent — the owners promised to “elevate the cocktail scene” — with pitchers for the table when the night is a group project.

2The room

Simplistically modern: dark tile and white marble at the bar, a pool table, seven or eight TVs running the games. Minimal on purpose, built for staying.

3The late menu

When the kitchen thins out, the rail keeps the essentials: pizza by the slice, wings and fried pickles off the fryolator, until 1 a.m. Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

By day it’s a family restaurant.

Same pies, same fryer, same marble — the late scene is what the room turns into on weekends, not what it is all the time.
