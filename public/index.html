Our story

Ten years of pizza at this address — and counting

The Pike comes from two Clinton families. Co-owner Joseph Giordano made New Haven-style pizza in this building for a decade; co-owner Jake Mastroianni grew up here and already runs Bacari Social in Westbrook. Together they rebuilt the old Chow space as the place Clinton stays late.
